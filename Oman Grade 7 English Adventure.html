Activity 2: Present Continuous for Future

Grammar Rule

Rule & Formula:

We use the Present Continuous (am/is/are + verb-ing) for future arrangements already planned and confirmed (e.g., "We're going to the aquarium tomorrow").

Drag the correct verb forms into the correct arrangement gaps:

1. Tomorrow, we _____ home.
(Hint: plural subject "we")
2. On Sunday, she _____ Al-Qurum Park.
(Hint: singular subject "she")
3. Tonight, we _____ a film.
(Hint: viewing action)
